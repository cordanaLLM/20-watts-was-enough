## Pre-release evidence and publication conditions

Publication requires the aggregate gate, an observed-complete deterministic
tag-bound PDF, checksum and attestation verification, and offline execution of
every admitted OCI digest to pass on the exact tag. The release-candidate tags
`v0.5.0-rc.N` remain GitHub draft pre-releases and are not archived. Zenodo
archives non-draft pre-releases too, so the draft state is required to avoid a
permanent DOI ([Decision 0102](../../decisions/0102-accept-release-candidate-tags.md)).
This source disclosure does not claim that a tag run, attestations or package
visibility checks have completed, or that the `ghcr.io/cordanallm/` packages are
public.

Known limits carried into this snapshot (issues checked open on 2026-10-16):

- the tracked semantic baseline's Poppler captures are still not re-captured,
  because the digest-pinned PDF-tools image is not yet published
  ([#20](https://github.com/cordanaLLM/20-watts-was-enough/issues/20));
- the PDF's tag structure and reading order are incomplete
  ([#17](https://github.com/cordanaLLM/20-watts-was-enough/issues/17)), so no
  accessibility conformance is claimed;
- an intermittent footer-only terminal page can make a render pair differ
  ([#122](https://github.com/cordanaLLM/20-watts-was-enough/issues/122));
- a one-second specialist budget can time out a shakedown test on a loaded
  runner ([#148](https://github.com/cordanaLLM/20-watts-was-enough/issues/148));
- the portal browser test can flake under local load
  ([#281](https://github.com/cordanaLLM/20-watts-was-enough/issues/281)).

These checks establish a reproducible publication boundary. They do not create
a scientific result or an independent review.
