# Research authority rules

These instructions extend root [`AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md).

- Preserve stable `C-`, `P-`, audit, source, and test identities.
- Claim promotion requires reciprocal links among ledger, its sources,
  affected concept, principle bundle, and protocol when claim is testable.
- Read primary source closely enough to record population or substrate,
  intervention, comparator, endpoint, uncertainty, and limitations. Do not
  promote from abstract, snippet, or secondary summary alone.
- Keep contrary evidence, corrections, retractions, and boundary failures.
- Deduplicate mechanisms by causal invariant and discriminating intervention,
  not by similar terminology or outcome.
- Use EU/Germany applicability workflow for normative language. Record
  official source, status, version, dates, actor, system, and jurisdiction.
- Apply [`research-integrity-baseline.md`](research-integrity-baseline.md) to
  claim-eligible outputs and every triggered ethics, rights, safety, misuse,
  collaboration, review, correction, or data-stewardship boundary.
- Disclose contributors and roles, accountable approval, funding and material
  support, competing interests, and material AI, automation, or external-service
  use at authority output claims.
- Required ethics or competent external approval precedes affected
  collection, intervention, release, or execution. Repository review is not a
  substitute.
- Do not add third-party source bodies unless redistribution is explicitly
  permitted and recorded. Prefer link-only provenance.
- Every number has claim ID, derivation, or explicit hypothesis label.
- Run documentation, source-boundary, coverage, taxonomy, and math validators
  appropriate to files changed.
