## Question and hypotheses

The fixture asks two different questions.

1. **Track A — source reproduction:** under claim-specific biological
   preparations, do the registered interventions reproduce the bounded
   direction, state distinction, and minimum effect declared below when the
   animal, donor, batch, tissue, repertoire, and assay are the independent units
   actually justified by the design?
2. **Track B — systems frontier:** at equal information, action, authority,
   reserve, compute, storage, human-work, time, and lifecycle-energy budgets,
   does an explicitly typed lifecycle reduce false deletion and unsafe
   reactivation beyond a complete conventional lifecycle with equivalent state
   capacity and controls?

Track B has two nested hypotheses. For C-746, the complete typed lifecycle must
improve both protected error rates in at least one task family without worsening
the hard safety and service gates. For C-747, it must retain a Pareto improvement
in **both** materially different task families, produce every registered
state-collapse failure signature, and beat the isomorphic ordinary-lifecycle
null. Merely renaming ordinary states with immune terms is a mandatory negative
control, not evidence.

## Systems, scenarios, and task families

### Track A — bounded source-domain reproductions

Each source module is a separate preregistered study. A module can pass or fail
without changing another module's verdict. Animals, human participants, primary
cells, tissue preparations, imaging fields, sequences, and repeated time points
are never pooled as interchangeable independent replicates. The experimental
unit is stated in each module below; nested measurements remain nested.

New animal or human work may start only with the applicable German and EU
authority, ethics, welfare, biosafety, and data-protection gates satisfied.
Approval is permission to run the registered study, not evidence for its claim.
No animal or participant may be enrolled solely to strengthen an AI analogy.
Where a qualified public raw dataset can answer the exact module, sealed
reanalysis precedes new collection.

### Track B — two non-interchangeable AI task families

1. **F1: dependency-constrained modular service.** A synthetic but
   event-sourced service contains 256 modules, versioned dependencies, scoped
   authorities, repairable and irreparable faults, compromised modules,
   misleading alarms, reserve instances, recurrence, and delayed verification.
   The protected action is whether a module may execute, remain suppressed,
   enter repair, return, contract to reserve, or be irreversibly retired.
2. **F2: continual evidence-worker pool.** A retrieval-and-tool pool contains
   128 versioned workers and an immutable evidence base. Tasks recur after
   distribution shifts; feedback can be correct, mistaken, or poisoned; local
   placement changes latency; workers can become stale, exhausted, repaired, or
   superseded. The protected action is whether capability is authorized,
   suppressed, relearned, recalled, locally resident, rolled back, or deleted.

For each seed and family, the generator freezes hidden ground truth, observation
history, event order, intervention opportunities, recurrence schedule, service
loss, and counterfactual repair outcome before any arm runs. An arm sees only
causally available observations. The independent AI unit is one
`seed × family × environment-version`; events within it are repeated measures.

## Arms, baselines, and strongest nulls

### Track A comparators

Every source module includes the cited preparation, its registered negative or
sham control, an assay-qualified positive control, and the strongest
interpretive null listed in its module card. The positive control can invalidate
an assay but cannot make the target contrast pass. Personnel performing
allocation, primary measurement, and locked analysis remain blinded to coded
arm identity wherever the intervention permits.

### Track B arms

1. **B0 — anomaly + IAM + recovery:** a tuned anomaly detector, calibrated
   risk and abstention, least-privilege IAM, quarantine, rollback, repair, and
   ordinary incident workflow.
2. **B1 — explicit ordinary state machine:** B0 plus versioned states,
   transition guards, timeouts, audit history, and a cost-sensitive policy.
3. **B2 — mature operational stack:** B1 plus resource-aware supervisory
   control, replay, recurrence monitoring, placement, reserve management,
   staged verification, and human escalation.
4. **B3 — isomorphic ordinary lifecycle, strongest null:** B2 plus the same
   state cardinality, transition capacity, memory fields, placement state,
   recovery state, parameter count, and tuning budget as the proposed arm. Its
   state names and derivation are conventional. If B3 reproduces the proposed
   decisions and frontier, the claimed immune-derived residual is rejected.
5. **T — typed lifecycle:** representation, recognition, authorization,
   activation, ignorance, anergy-like suppression, active external
   suppression, exhaustion, deletion, expansion, contraction, memory,
   residency, recovery, and resource state are separately versioned and linked
   to different permissible actions and reversibility contracts.
6. **L — label-only negative control:** byte-identical B3 decisions and state
   graph with immune vocabulary substituted only in reports. L must equal B3
   exactly; any measured advantage is analysis leakage.
7. **O — evaluator oracle:** hidden truth chooses the minimum registered loss
   action. O estimates headroom and never competes for promotion.

B3 is the decisive mature null. Winning against B0–B2 but tying B3 rejects the
novel systems claim while retaining any ordinary engineering lesson.

## Equal budgets and cost boundary

### Track A matched biological budgets

Within a source module, randomized arms receive the same componentwise ceilings
for independent units, sex and age strata where relevant, litters or donors,
sampling times, viable cells, tissue mass (`mg`), imaging duration (`s`), fields,
sequencing reads, assay plates, reagent lots, operator-hours (`h`), instrument
time (`s`), stored bytes (`B`), and analysis CPU time (`s`). The target and
strongest null share the same assay depth and missing-data rule. A saved sample,
read, field, or operator-hour is recorded and cannot be donated after outcomes
are visible.

The minimum number of independent units is set before allocation from a blinded
variance or event-rate estimate:

$$
n_{\mathrm{arm}}=\left\lceil
\frac{2\left(z_{1-\alpha^\star/2}+z_{0.90}\right)^2\hat\sigma^2}
{\delta_m^2}
\right\rceil,
$$

where $n_{\mathrm{arm}}$ is the independent-unit count per arm,
$\alpha^\star=0.05/39$ is the conservative two-sided planning rate for the
39 frozen Track A component contrasts,
$\hat\sigma$ is the blinded pooled standard deviation in that module's declared
measurement unit, and $\delta_m$ is its frozen minimum relevant effect. Binary,
count, survival, and nested endpoints use the corresponding exact or
simulation-based power model at 90% power. Holm, rather than Bonferroni, governs
the final familywise decisions. If the required count exceeds the
ethics-approved cap, the module is infeasible; the effect threshold or unit
definition is not relaxed.

### Track B matched AI budgets

Per `seed × family` and arm, the hard ceilings are:

- 50,000 decision opportunities and 5,000 permitted interventions;
- 2,000 supervised labels and 500 reviewer escalations;
- 30 reviewer-minutes, recorded by action and role;
- one pinned CPU core for at most 1,800 CPU-seconds;
- 8 GiB peak resident memory, 2 GiB persistent state, and 10 GiB total I/O;
- identical observation fields, evidence vintages, action vocabulary, authority,
  fallback, and 10% reserve capacity;
- 120 tuning trials per arm over development seeds only; and
- externally measured wall energy in joules, with the same machine, power mode,
  thermal window, idle measurement, and run-order counterbalancing.

An arm exceeding any ceiling is infeasible for that paired unit. Unused budget
is not converted into extra labels, intervention authority, reserve, tuning, or
review. Energy is reported as

$$
E_{\mathrm{net}}=E_{\mathrm{wall}}-P_{\mathrm{idle}}t,
$$

where $E_{\mathrm{wall}}$ is wall-plug energy in joules, $P_{\mathrm{idle}}$ is
the preregistered idle baseline in watts, and $t$ is elapsed seconds. Modeled
energy is diagnostic only and cannot satisfy this boundary.

## Measurements, units, and source-reproduction modules

All thresholds below are protocol decision constants, not established effect
sizes. Every confidence interval is 95% unless a stricter familywise interval is
specified. Passing a module reproduces only the bounded statement named by its
claim.

Before allocation, every module must add a source-specific SOP that freezes the
preparation, timeline, assay panel, exclusion rules, one primary contrast for
each conjunct in the linked claim, and the exact strongest-null statistic. All
registered primary contrasts across Track A enter one Holm family at 0.05; a
conjunctive claim passes only when **every** component contrast passes its
corrected interval and minimum effect. Every named strongest null is mandatory:
the corrected interval for its direct contrast must exclude no effect in the
claimed direction and meet the module's numeric minimum. Positive-control
success, an uncorrected choice among outcomes, or an `OR` across endpoints can
never pass a module. Until the SOP and registry receipt exist, the module is a
protocol-complete design, not a preregistered or executable study.

assay but cannot add, remove, or substitute a success endpoint:

### SR-727 — clonal deletion

- **Independent unit:** mouse, blocked by litter and sex; thymocytes and flow
  events are nested.
- **Intervention and strongest null:** receptor-transgenic mice with the
  deleting MHC context versus matched receptor-transgenic mice without that
  context; an MHC-only assay control checks marker specificity.
- **Primary measurements:** clonotype-positive mature thymocytes per $10^6$
  viable thymocytes (`cell/10^6 cell`) and their proportion (`1`).
- **Pass threshold:** upper interval for the risk ratio is at most `0.75` and
  the lower interval for absolute reduction is at least `0.20`.
- **Kill gate:** retire the module for unbalanced viability, litter-confounded
  allocation, post-hoc gates, or a control failure.

### SR-729 — persistence with functional silencing

- **Independent unit:** mouse; wells and stimulation repeats are nested.
- **Intervention and strongest null:** the double-transgenic preparation versus
  receptor-only and depletion-matched controls. The decisive null is that low
  response is explained solely by lower cell abundance.
- **Primary measurements:** self-reactive cells per $10^6$ splenocytes and the
  stimulation response ratio (`fold`) after equal viable-cell input.
- **Pass threshold:** the lower interval for persistence is at least `0.25` of
  receptor-only abundance while the upper interval for response is at most
  `0.50` of the abundance-matched control.
- **Kill gate:** fail if persistence and response cannot be measured in the same
  coded animal or if viability differs by more than 10 percentage points.

### SR-730 — active regulatory tolerance

- **Independent unit:** mouse, with depletion, sham, transfer/rescue, and
  Foxp3-competent controls randomized within litter.
- **Intervention and strongest null:** regulatory-cell depletion or
  Foxp3-disrupted development followed by registered rescue; the strongest
  null is intrinsic inactivity of the target population without active
  regulation.
- **Primary measurements:** autoimmune pathology incidence (`case/mouse`),
  blinded ordinal pathology, and activated self-reactive cells per $10^6$ cells.
- **Pass threshold:** rescue lowers pathology incidence by at least 20
  percentage points and activated-cell burden by at least 25%, with both
  intervals excluding no improvement.
- **Kill gate:** stop for severity beyond the approved humane endpoint, failed
  depletion/transfer verification, or unblinded pathology scoring.

### SR-731 — differentiated exhaustion program

- **Independent unit:** mouse; cells and genes are nested. Whole batches, not
  cells, are held out.
- **Intervention and strongest null:** chronic-LCMV virus-specific CD8 cells
  versus acute effector, memory, and registered anergy references matched for
  sorting, viability, batch, and library depth. The strongest null uses generic
  activation and expression-intensity covariates only.
- **Primary measurements:** raw molecular counts, held-batch macro-AUROC (`1`),
  log loss (`nat/cell`), and signed pathway effects.
- **Pass threshold:** lower interval for macro-AUROC is at least `0.80`, exceeds
  the intensity null by at least `0.10`, and the direction of the locked pathway
  panel agrees in at least 75% of independent batches.
- **Kill gate:** fail for cell-level train/test leakage, post-outcome gene-panel
  selection, unresolved batch/condition aliasing, or fewer than three
  independent batches per state.

### SR-733 — expansion, contraction, memory, and recall

- **Independent unit:** mouse; destructive time-point cohorts are separate
  randomized animals.
- **Intervention and strongest null:** homologous primary challenge and later
  rechallenge versus sham and non-homologous challenge; a one-state
  rise-and-decay model is the strongest trajectory null.
- **Primary measurements:** antigen-specific CD8 cells per $10^6$ cells,
  elapsed days, peak expansion (`fold`), contraction fraction (`1`), retained
  memory (`cell/10^6 cell`), and recall expansion (`fold`).
- **Pass threshold:** at least `4×` primary expansion, at least `0.50`
  contraction from peak, memory above the prechallenge upper interval, and at
  least `2×` expansion from the prerechallenge memory level after homologous
  recall.
- **Kill gate:** fail if time points, precursor denominators, or tissues differ
  by arm without preregistration.

### SR-734 — help-qualified germinal-centre loop

- **Independent unit:** mouse; tracked cells, contacts, and imaging frames are
  nested.
- **Intervention and strongest null:** experimentally varied antigen capture or
  T-cell help with blinded intravital tracking. The strongest null is an
  intensity- and motility-matched Markov path without help-conditioned return.
- **Primary measurements:** help-contact duration (`s`), zone-transition latency
  (`s`), return probability (`1`), and divisions per tracked lineage (`count`).
- **Pass threshold:** high-help cells have a return-and-division risk ratio of at
  least `1.50`, with the lower interval above `1.00`, after the locked motility
  and imaging-quality adjustments.
- **Kill gate:** fail for hand-selected tracks, arm-dependent field duration,
  unresolved phototoxicity, or mouse identity omitted from uncertainty.

### SR-735 — multilineage affinity maturation

- **Independent unit:** mouse or independent germinal centre when centres are
  prospectively isolated; sequences within it are nested.
- **Intervention and strongest null:** lineage-resolved sampling across the
  registered maturation interval. The strongest null is a depth-matched
  winner-take-all birth–death model fitted before lineage labels are revealed.
- **Primary measurements:** Hill diversity $N_2$ (`effective lineage count`),
  lineage abundance (`1`), binding affinity in the registered assay unit, and
  improvement from baseline (`1`).
- **Pass threshold:** late-stage $N_2\ge2.0$ in the lower interval while median
  affinity improves by at least 20%; at least three lineages must cross the
  predeclared nontrivial-abundance threshold in a majority of independent units.
- **Kill gate:** fail if PCR families are counted as lineages, sequencing depth
  differs by arm, or apparent diversity vanishes under the locked error model.

### SR-736 — sustained trained innate response

- **Independent unit:** mouse for the animal arm and human participant for the
  volunteer/ex-vivo arm; wells and repeated challenges are nested.
- **Intervention and strongest null:** registered fungal or BCG exposure versus
  sham, with a delayed heterologous challenge and lymphocyte-depleted or
  lineage-qualified sensitivity analysis. The strongest null is residual
  exposure material or acute inflammation without persistent state change.
- **Primary measurements:** delay (`day`), cytokine concentration (`pg/mL`),
  viable-cell-normalized response (`pg/10^6 cell`), and clinical or welfare
  events (`event/person` or `event/mouse`).
- **Pass threshold:** the delayed heterologous response differs by at least 25%
  with its corrected interval excluding zero in both one animal/cell
  preparation and one human-participant preparation. After the registered
  lymphocyte depletion or lineage exclusion, a residual difference of at least
  20% must remain with its corrected interval excluding zero in each preparation.
- **Kill gate:** stop for serious adverse events, unapproved secondary use,
  arm-dependent challenge dose, evidence of ongoing exposure, or participant-
  level leakage across splits.

### SR-737 — multiple trained-immunity substrates

- **Independent unit:** donor or mouse; cells, loci, metabolites, and progeny
  are nested.
- **Intervention and strongest null:** trained versus sham exposure with locked
  chromatin, metabolic, progenitor, and functional panels; vehicle and
  state-disrupting interventions form the null stack.
- **Primary measurements:** accessibility or modification effect (`log2 fold`),
  metabolite abundance (`mol` or normalized assay unit), progenitor frequency
  (`cell/10^6 cell`), and heterologous functional response (`pg/mL`).
- **Pass threshold:** epigenetic, metabolic, and hematopoietic-progenitor
  contrasts must each reproduce their single locked direction in the
  source-matched preparation with corrected intervals excluding zero. A locked
  intervention in each substrate class must reduce its associated functional
  response difference by at least 20% without a greater than
  10-percentage-point viability loss.
- **Kill gate:** fail for purely correlational substrate selection after seeing
  function, batch–arm aliasing, or intervention toxicity beyond the gate.

### SR-739 — antigen-independent memory maintenance

- **Independent unit:** mouse; transferred cells and assay wells are nested.
- **Intervention and strongest null:** memory cells maintained without cognate
  antigen under registered cytokine-support perturbations versus antigen-
  present and support-intact controls. The strongest null attributes persistence
  to hidden antigen carryover.
- **Primary measurements:** memory cells per $10^6$ cells, survival time (`day`),
  homeostatic divisions (`count/cell`), and recall response (`fold`).
- **Pass threshold:** antigen-free support retains memory above the assay lower
  limit at day 28, while cytokine-support removal lowers retained count or
  recall by at least 30% with its interval excluding zero.
- **Kill gate:** fail for detectable cognate antigen in the antigen-free arm,
  unequal transfer viability, or unregistered homeostatic support.

### SR-740 — generated and selected receptor diversity

- **Independent unit:** donor or mouse repertoire; sequences are nested and
  paired productive/nonproductive samples remain grouped.
- **Intervention and strongest null:** a frozen rearrangement-plus-selection
  generator is compared with depth-matched empirical-frequency, rearrangement-
  only, and label-permuted models on held-out repertoires.
- **Primary measurements:** unique sequences (`count`), sampling depth
  (`read`), per-sequence log likelihood (`nat/sequence`), calibration error
  (`1`), Hill diversity (`effective clonotype count`), and rarefaction curves.
- **Pass threshold:** the complete generator improves held-out log likelihood by
  at least `0.10 nat/sequence` over the best mature null and retains calibrated
  abundance quantiles across at least three preregistered sampling depths.
- **Kill gate:** fail for sequence-level rather than repertoire-level splitting,
  productive/nonproductive leakage, undocumented clone collapsing, or depth-
  dependent ranking reversal outside the registered uncertainty.

### SR-741 — tissue-resident memory and local protection

- **Independent unit:** mouse, blocked by tissue and experiment batch.
- **Intervention and strongest null:** resident-cell retention or depletion with
  circulation qualification by the registered lab method, compared with a
  circulation-matched memory control. The strongest null is rapid recruitment
  from circulation rather than local initiation.
- **Primary measurements:** resident and circulating cells per tissue, response
  latency (`h`), local burden (`log10 registered unit`), tissue damage (blinded
  scale), and maintenance duration (`day`).
- **Pass threshold:** qualified resident cells must persist above the frozen
  non-equilibrium residence threshold **and** reduce response latency by at
  least 25% versus the circulation-matched null, with both corrected intervals
  passing. Local burden must fall by at least 0.5 log10 unit as a required
  co-primary protection endpoint; resident depletion must remove at least half
  of both latency and burden benefits.
- **Kill gate:** fail for incomplete circulation qualification, tissue-dependent
  assay depth, unblinded damage scoring, or a control population mismatch.

### SR-742 — metabolic or mitochondrial intervention

- **Independent unit:** mouse or primary-cell donor; wells and repeated assays
  are nested.
- **Intervention and strongest null:** locked metabolic or mitochondrial
  perturbations in effector, regulatory, or memory differentiation, with
  vehicle, viability-matched stress, and generic proliferation controls.
- **Primary measurements:** viable cells (`count`), ATP or metabolite amount
  (`mol` or registered assay unit), respiration (`pmol O2/min`), phenotype
  fraction (`1`), and task-specific functional response (`registered unit`).
- **Pass threshold:** the three primary functional outcomes and directions are
  fixed here: viable-cell-normalized effector cytokine output must decrease by
  at least 20% under the source-matched glycolytic perturbation; suppression of
  responder proliferation by the regulatory preparation must decrease by at
  least 20% under its source-matched oxidative-metabolism perturbation; and
  equal-precursor memory recall expansion must increase by at least 20% under
  the source-matched memory-promoting fatty-acid/mitochondrial intervention.
  Each corrected interval must exclude zero after viability and proliferation
  matching, and each direction must reproduce in a second independent batch or
  site. The two-site value is the larger valid paired-test value.
- **Kill gate:** fail when toxicity, proliferation, batch, or assay saturation
  alone explains the functional effect, or when resource and functional
  measurements come from incomparable populations.

## AI outcomes and units

Track B preserves rather than scalarizes these outcome families:

1. **False deletion:** benign-capable modules irreversibly removed per 1,000
   eligible module-episodes.
2. **Unsafe reactivation:** unrepaired harmful modules returned to authorized
   active state per 1,000 reactivation opportunities.
3. **Unauthorized activation and missed harm:** events per 10,000 protected
   action opportunities, with a one-sided 95% exact upper bound.
4. **State errors:** recognition, authorization, suppression, exhaustion,
   deletion, memory, residency, and recovery confusion counts per 1,000 true
   transitions.
5. **Service:** accepted task fraction (`1`), protected task loss (`1`),
   availability (`%`), recurrence (`event/10,000 opportunity`), and retained
   capability after delay (`%`).
6. **Recovery:** detection, containment, verified repair, and safe-return
   latency in events and seconds; censored failures remain in survival analysis.
7. **Calibration:** Brier score (`1`), expected calibration error (`1`), and
   abstention/selective-risk curves by event type and family.
8. **Resources:** CPU time (`s`), wall time (`s`), p50/p95/p99 action latency
   (`ms`), peak memory (`B`), persistent state (`B`), I/O (`B`), reserve use
   (`module-event`), reviewer work (`min`), and net wall energy (`J`).

The primary paired effects for arm $a$ against B3 are

$$
\Delta_{FD,a}=r_{FD,a}-r_{FD,B3},\qquad
\Delta_{UR,a}=r_{UR,a}-r_{UR,B3},
$$

where $r_{FD}$ is false deletions per 1,000 eligible module-episodes and
$r_{UR}$ is unsafe reactivations per 1,000 opportunities. Negative differences
favour the tested arm. Denominators are frozen from hidden ground truth before
arm execution; abstention cannot remove an opportunity.

saved resources are logged and not reassigned.

3. collapse exhaustion and resource shortage;
4. collapse active response, contraction, and memory;

## Analysis and statistical plan

### Track A

1. Lock the protocol, allocation manifest, assay panel, primary endpoint,
   independent unit, exclusion rule, minimum effect, and analysis container
   before codes are released.
2. Analyse every module separately. Continuous endpoints use the registered
   hierarchical model or blocked paired contrast; binary endpoints use exact or
   generalized mixed models; counts retain exposure; survival retains censoring
   and competing events.
3. Holm controls familywise error across the complete frozen vector of Track A
   primary component and strongest-null contrasts at 0.05. A module verdict is
   the conjunction of its corrected required contrasts. Secondary outcomes and
   mechanistic mediation remain labelled exploratory.
4. Report raw-unit effects, 95% familywise intervals, every independent unit,
   batch and site effects, missingness, exclusions, welfare events, deviations,
   and the strongest-null result. A $p$ value alone cannot pass a module.
5. A multisite claim requires the locked direction in at least two independent
   sites or batches. Pooling cannot conceal a sign reversal.
6. The confirmatory packet is immutable. A failed assay may trigger a new
   versioned study, never retroactive replacement of the failed run.

1. Use development seeds only for generator diagnostics, baseline tuning,
   hyperparameters, and the 120-trial budget. Freeze code and container before
   confirmation.
2. Recompute all metrics from append-only raw events. Verify sequence numbers,
   environment and arm hashes, causal availability, action authority, budget
   equality, and energy provenance before analysis.
3. Estimate paired seed-level effects. Count rates use paired hierarchical
   negative-binomial or exact models with exposure; service fractions use
   paired binomial models; recovery uses survival analysis; calibration reports
   uncertainty by family and regime.
4. Holm controls the four primary comparisons—false deletion and unsafe
